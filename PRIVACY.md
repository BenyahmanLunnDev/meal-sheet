# Privacy Policy for Meal Sheet

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

Meal Sheet is a free, open-source app made by [@BenyahmanLunnDev](https://github.com/BenyahmanLunnDev). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

**Short version:** Meal Sheet does not collect, upload, share, or sell any of your information. It has no ads, no analytics, and no account, and it does not have permission to access the internet.

## What the app stores

The app saves the following **only on your device**:

- the name and initials you enter
- your meal prices and pay schedule
- the meals you log (the date, which meal, the time you logged it, and the price)

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

## Sharing and exporting

If you tap **Share** or **Export**, the app passes a text summary or CSV file to the app you choose (for example Messages or Gmail). What happens after that is between you and that app.

## Device backups

If Android's device backup is turned on, Android may include Meal Sheet's data in your personal Google backup, as it does for other apps. That's controlled by your device settings, not by Meal Sheet, and the developer can't see it.

## Deleting your data

Use **Settings → Clear all meals**, or uninstall the app, to delete everything it has stored.

## Children

Meal Sheet is meant for working adults and does not collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new date.
